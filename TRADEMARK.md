### The marks

"Éclair CMS"™, "Éclair"™ as applied to the software, and the Éclair logo are trademarks of Thierry Gustin. Registration is pending; until it is granted, the marks are shown with ™, not ®.

### The license covers the code, not the name

Éclair CMS is free software released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`NOTICE`](NOTICE)). That license gives you rights over the **code**. It gives you no rights over the **name** or the **logo**: section 7(e) of the license explicitly allows a project to decline to grant rights under trademark law. This policy explains what you may do with the name without asking.

### What you may do without asking

- **Say that a site runs on Éclair CMS**, for example "Powered by Éclair CMS" or "Built with Éclair CMS".
- **Write about Éclair CMS**: articles, reviews, tutorials, videos, books and training courses.
- **Make and sell themes, extensions or services for Éclair CMS**, and describe them as "compatible with Éclair CMS" or "for Éclair CMS", as long as their own name does not contain "Éclair" or "Eclair" and nothing suggests they are official.
- **Distribute the unmodified software** under its name, for example in a hosting provider's one-click installer, with the copyright and license notices intact.

### What needs written permission

- Using "Éclair" or "Eclair" in the **name of a fork, a product, a hosting service or a SaaS**, including variations, translations and look-alike spellings.
- Using the **Éclair logo**, or a logo that could be confused with it.
- Suggesting an **official partnership**, certification, sponsorship or endorsement by Éclair CMS or Thierry Gustin.
- Using a **domain name** that contains "eclaircms" or "eclair-cms", or a social media account or app store name built around the marks.

### Forks

You are free to fork Éclair CMS under the terms of the AGPL. A fork that is distributed or offered to others must **change its name and logo**. You may say, factually, that it is "based on Éclair CMS".

### Contact

To ask for a trademark license or for permission for a use not covered here, write to Thierry Gustin: thierry.gustin@gmail.com.
